_Last updated: 11 November 2026_

Similynk makes **Ship** and **Muso**: the Android apps and the websites at ship.similynk.com, muso.similynk.com and www.similynk.com. This page explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't use advertising or analytics trackers.

## What we collect

**Your account.** When you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and profile photo. If you sign up with an email and password instead, we store your email; your password is stored only as a secure hash by our sign-in provider. One Similynk account works in both Ship and Muso.

**What you add to your profile.** Your name, photo, banner and bio, and, if you choose to add them, your birthday, anniversary and phone number.

**Your family and circles.** The relatives you add (including people added by name only), how you are related, and the circles you create or join.

**What you share in Ship.** Messages, photos and voice messages in chats, reactions, posts, comments and likes, memories and milestones, and anything you report to us.

**Your music in Muso.** Liked songs, albums and artists, playlists, play history and counts, your listening streak, the listening rooms you create or join, and issue reports you file.

**How you use the apps.** Which Similynk apps you use and when you last opened them, and, if you leave them switched on, when you were last online and whether you have read a message. You can turn last seen and read receipts off in Settings.

**On your phone.** Your settings, saved copies of chats, family and profiles so they open without internet, and songs you download. These stay on your device and are removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall it.

## Why we use it

- To run the apps: sign you in, deliver your messages, keep your library in sync and show your profile.
- To show your content only to the people you choose: family, circles, followers or everyone, depending on your settings.
- To keep people safe: blocking, reports and Legacy (see below).
- To send you notifications on your phone while the app is open or running in the background.

## Who can see it

Your posts, profile details and connections follow the visibility you choose. Chats are seen only by the people in them. Nobody can find you by searching your name: someone needs your exact email or phone number, or a place in your family or circles.

## Services we use

- **Supabase** stores your account, data and media, and handles sign-in.
- **Vercel** hosts the websites.
- **Google** provides Google sign-in.
- **YouTube** supplies the music in Muso. When you play a song, it is requested from YouTube.
- **LRCLIB** supplies song lyrics. Muso sends it the song's title and artist, nothing about you.

These providers only process data to run the service for us.

## Legacy

In Ship you can choose family who can look after your profile if you pass away. If your account is marked as remembered, it stays visible to your family as a memorial, or is deleted if that is what you chose. If it happened by mistake, you can come back.

## Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account exists. Some things clear themselves: notifications after 90 days, and listening rooms when they expire.

- **Download your data** from your page in Ship or Muso (tap your picture).
- **Delete your account** from the same page. This permanently deletes your profile, chats, posts, photos, family links and library in both apps. See [Delete your account](/docs/delete-account/).

Deleted data may remain in our provider's backups for a short time before it is overwritten.

## Children

Ship and Muso are not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

## Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Questions or requests: **hello@similynk.com**.
