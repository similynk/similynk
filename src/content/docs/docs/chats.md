title: Chats

---

You can message your family and anyone in your circles. To message someone else, type their exact email or phone number. Nobody can find you by searching your name. Every circle also has a **group chat** for everyone in it.

## Messages

- Send text, **photos** and **voice messages** (hold the mic, up to 2 minutes). Songs shared from Similynk Music play in Music when you tap them.
- **Reply** by swiping a message to the right (or hovering it on the web). Tap the quote to jump to the original.
- **React** with an emoji, **edit** your message within 15 minutes, or **delete it for everyone**.
- **Forward** any message to one or more of your chats, and **copy** its text.
- Web addresses in messages open when you tap them.
- Scroll up to load older messages, as far back as the chat goes.

## Finding things

Open a chat's <kbd>⋮</kbd> menu and choose **Search** to find words anywhere in that chat. Step through the matches with the arrows.

## Keeping your list tidy

Hold a chat in your list (or use its <kbd>⋮</kbd> menu) to:

- **Pin** it to the top, up to five chats.
- **Mute** it, so it stops sending pop-ups and phone notifications. Its unread count turns grey.
- **Clear** it, which removes its messages for you only.
- **Delete** a one-to-one chat, which clears it and removes it from your list until they write again.

A message you started but didn't send stays as a **Draft** on that chat. Pins, mutes and drafts are kept on each phone or browser for now.

## Seen, typing and last seen

You see when someone is typing, when they've read your message (✓✓) and when they were last online. Turn off **Read receipts** or **Last seen** in **Settings → Privacy**; you then won't see other people's either.
