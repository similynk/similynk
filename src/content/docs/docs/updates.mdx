## Music

### 2.3 · August 2026

- <Badge text="Added" variant="success" /> Trending page — discover the most-played and most-liked songs, albums, and artists across every Similynk Music listener, with Today / All Time / Most Liked / and Most Replayed views.
- <Badge text="Added" variant="success" /> Play-count and like-count badges on trending cards.
- <Badge text="Improved" variant="note" /> Settings now uses a horizontal tab bar instead of a sidebar, matching the rest of the app.
- <Badge text="Improved" variant="note" /> Sidebar icons stay centered in both expanded and collapsed states.
- <Badge text="Fixed" variant="caution" /> Repeated library imports no longer inflate lifetime play counts.

### 2.2 · June 2026

- <Badge text="Added" variant="success" /> Listening streaks — a flame badge tracks your current run of consecutive listening days.
- <Badge text="Added" variant="success" /> Lifetime stats on Home — liked songs, playlists, and total plays at a glance.
- <Badge text="Added" variant="success" /> Share any song via a direct link, using the native share sheet on mobile.
- <Badge text="Improved" variant="note" /> Account avatar now shows your real profile photo instead of initials, when one is set.

### 2.1 · April 2026

- <Badge text="Added" variant="success" /> Installable PWA — add Similynk Music to your home screen on desktop and mobile.
- <Badge text="Added" variant="success" /> Offline app-shell caching for instant loads on repeat visits.
- <Badge text="Added" variant="success" /> Keyboard shortcuts for playback, library, and navigation.
- <Badge text="Fixed" variant="caution" /> Occasional stale cached assets after an update — the service worker now clears itself outside production.

- <Badge text="Improved" variant="note" /> Queue management — reorder by drag, remove individual tracks, clear in one tap.
