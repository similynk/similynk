## Music

### 2.5 · October 2026

- <Badge text="Added" variant="success" /> Send in a chat: share any song to your Similynk Messenger chats.
- <Badge text="Added" variant="success" /> Songs opened from Messenger play right away, or wait with a Play now button if you're already listening.
- <Badge text="Added" variant="success" /> Message on a profile opens your chat in Messenger.
- <Badge text="Improved" variant="note" /> Playback recovers by itself when a song stalls or a stream link expires.
- <Badge text="Improved" variant="note" /> The app reopens where you left it from the media notification.
- <Badge text="Fixed" variant="caution" /> The Similynk Music logo no longer wraps onto two lines.

### 2.4 · October 2026

- <Badge text="Added" variant="success" /> One Similynk account — sign in with Google and your likes, playlists and history stay in sync between the web and the app.
- <Badge text="Added" variant="success" /> Profiles on the web — your banner, listening streak, followers, following and connections, and the music you share.
- <Badge text="Added" variant="success" /> Listening rooms work across the web and the app, joined with an 8-character invite code.
- <Badge text="Added" variant="success" /> More reactions on issue reports, including ➕ for "me too" and 🗿.
- <Badge text="Improved" variant="note" /> Trending counts plays from both the web and the app, with Today, All Time and Most Liked views.
- <Badge text="Improved" variant="note" /> Play history is kept for 7, 10, 30 or 50 days on the web and the app.
- <Badge text="Improved" variant="note" /> A new name and logo: PlayMuso is now Similynk Music.
- <Badge text="Improved" variant="note" /> Lyric timing nudges stay on your device; shared lyric corrections have been retired.

- <Badge text="Improved" variant="note" /> Settings now uses a horizontal tab bar instead of a sidebar, matching the rest of the app.
