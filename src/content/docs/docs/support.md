## Get help in the app

In **Ship** or **Muso**, open **Settings › Help**. You'll find guides, and you can report a problem or suggest an idea. You can follow what happens to your report there, and see what others have reported.

## Contact us

Email **hello@similynk.com** about anything: a problem, a question about your data, or a safety concern. We read every message.

## Common questions

- **Installing the Android apps:** see [Installing](/docs/installing/).
- **What's changed:** see [Updates](/docs/updates/).
- **Your data:** see the [Privacy policy](/docs/privacy/).
- **Deleting your account:** see [Delete your account](/docs/delete-account/).
- **Someone is bothering you:** block them from the ⋮ menu in your chat with them, then report it from **Settings › Help** or by email.
- **A child may be at risk:** see our [Child safety standards](/docs/child-safety/).
