---
title: Settings
description: Appearance, notifications and privacy.
---

Open **Settings** from the bottom bar in the app, or the sidebar on the web.

## Appearance

Choose **Dark**, **Light** or **System** (follows your phone or computer), and one of 17 **accent colours**. Messenger and Music offer the same themes and colours, so you can match both apps.

## Notifications

The Messenger app shows a phone notification for new messages and activity (family requests, circle invites, likes and comments) while it's open or running in the background. Tap one to go straight to that chat. Muted chats stay quiet. On the web, allow notifications in your browser to get pop-ups.

## Privacy

Turn **Read receipts** and **Last seen** on or off, and manage the people you've blocked.
