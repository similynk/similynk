_Last updated: 19 November 2026_

These standards apply to **Ship** and **Muso**, on Android and on the web.

## Zero tolerance

Similynk does not allow child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) of any kind. This includes child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sexualising minors, and any attempt to contact a child for sexual purposes. Ship and Muso are not meant for children under 13.

## What we do

- We remove CSAE content as soon as we find it or are told about it.
- We close the accounts involved and keep them from coming back.
- We report CSAM and other CSAE to the relevant authorities, such as India's National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal and the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC), as the law requires.
- We keep the information the authorities need, and otherwise delete it.

## How to report a concern

- **In the app:** open **Settings › Help** in Ship or Muso and report it. You can also block the person from the ⋮ menu in your chat with them.
- **By email:** write to **hello@similynk.com**. Include the person's name or username and what happened, if you can.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first.

## Our child safety contact

Questions about these standards, or about how we handle reports, go to **hello@similynk.com**. This is our designated point of contact for child safety, including for Google Play.

## Laws we follow

We comply with the child safety laws that apply to us, including India's Information Technology Act and the POCSO Act, and the laws of the countries where Ship and Muso are available.
