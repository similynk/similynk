---
title: Getting started
description: What Similynk is and how to begin.
---

Similynk is one account for the people you're close to. Sign in with Google and you're the same person everywhere you use it: same name, photo and connections.

You use it through **Ship**, where your words are shipped to your “ships”, and **Muso**, for all your ad-free music, free. Start with either.
