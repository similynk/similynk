---
title: Delete your account
description: How to delete your Similynk account and everything in it, from Ship or Muso.
---

Your Similynk account is shared by Ship and Muso, so deleting it removes it from both.

## In the app or on the web

1. Open **Ship** or **Muso** and sign in.
2. Tap your **picture** at the top to open your page.
3. Scroll to the bottom and choose **Delete account**.
4. Confirm. It happens straight away.

Want a copy first? On the same page, choose **Download your data** before you delete.

## What gets deleted

Everything linked to your account, permanently:

- your profile, photo, banner and bio
- your chats, messages, photos and voice messages
- your posts, comments, likes and memories
- your family links and circles
- your Muso library, playlists, history and streak
- your sign-in details

Messages you sent stay deleted for everyone. Deleted data may remain in our provider's backups for a short time before it is overwritten.

## Can't sign in?

Email **hello@similynk.com** from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We'll confirm and delete it within 30 days.

See the [privacy policy](/docs/privacy/) for what we keep and why.
