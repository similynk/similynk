---
title: Profile
description: What your profile shows, and how to change it.
---

Your profile shows your name, photo, banner, bio, followers and connections, and the music you've chosen to share. People see how they're connected to you, like family or a shared circle.

- Tap the **camera** on your photo to take or choose a new one.
- Tap the **pencil** on your banner to pick one of the preset banners.
- Edit your **bio** from your profile.

Your profile is the same in Messenger and Music: change it in one and the other shows it too. From someone's profile you can **message** them, or play a song they share in Similynk Music.
