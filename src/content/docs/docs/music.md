---

Listen to any song, keep liked songs, albums and playlists, and open a **listening room** to play in sync with others, on the web or the app, using its 8-character invite code. What you like can appear on your profile; you decide how much and who sees it.

## Sharing with Messenger

- Open a song's menu and choose **Send in a chat** to send it to any of your Messenger chats.
- Tap a shared song in Messenger and it opens in Music. If nothing is playing it starts; if you're already listening to something, it opens with a **Play now** button so it doesn't cut in.
- **Message** on someone's Music profile opens your chat with them in Messenger.
