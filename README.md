## One account, every app

### 💬 Similynk Messenger

A private space for the people you actually know.

- **Your family, mapped.** Build your family tree together. Every relationship
  is confirmed by both people, and only your family can see who's in it.
- **Circles for everyone else.** School, college, work and friends each get
  their own circle, joined with a short invite code.
- **Chats that keep up.** One-to-one and group chats with photos, voice
  messages, replies, reactions and songs shared straight from Music.
- **Share with exactly who you mean.** Post moments to your family, your whole
  tree or a single circle. Nothing goes further than you decide.

### 🎵 Similynk Music

Free music streaming, ad free, that remembers you and the people you listen with.

- **Plays everywhere.** Background playback, lock-screen controls, a queue you
  can reorder, a sleep timer and synced lyrics.
- **Listen together.** Listening Rooms keep everyone on the same song at the
  same moment, joined with an invite code.
- **Discover what's next.** Trending songs, moods and genres, and autoplay
  radio when your queue runs out.
- **A profile that shows your taste.** Liked songs, playlists, favourite
  artists, your 🔥 listening streak and the music you have in common with a
  friend.

---

## Why Similynk is different

- **Only people you know.** Nobody can find you by name alone. People reach you
  through your family, your circles, or an email address they already have.
- **You decide who sees what.** Every post, circle and profile section has its
  own audience.
- **Nothing to scroll past.** No ads, no strangers, no feed tuned to keep you
  hooked.
- **One profile everywhere.** Sign in once with Google and your name, photo,
  connections and music follow you across every Similynk app.

### Installing on Android

1. On your phone, open [similynk.com](https://www.similynk.com/#download) and
   tap the app you want.
2. Open the downloaded file and allow the install when asked.
3. If Play Protect warns you, tap **More details**, then **Install anyway**.
4. Open the app and tap **Continue with Google**.

On an older phone, if the app won't install, get the 32-bit version from the
[releases page](https://github.com/similynk/similynk/releases).
